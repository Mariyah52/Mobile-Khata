Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mobile Khata ("the app", "we") is a business-management web app by IMADI Tech, Karachi, Pakistan. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, password (stored hashed by Firebase Authentication, never visible to us) and the name/phone of your company.
  • Business data you enter: quotations, challans, invoices, purchases, customers, suppliers, products and stock, payments, expenses and related records, plus company branding (logo, stamp, signature) if you add them.
  • Technical data: basic error and activity logs for your company, used to keep the service working.

How it is used

Only to provide the app to you: to store and show your records, generate your documents and let your team members you invite access them. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting). Each company's data is kept in its own separate area and is accessible only to that company's members, enforced by server-side security rules. Platform administrators can see company profile and subscription information needed to run the service.

Voice commands

If you use the voice-command feature, speech is processed by your device/browser's speech-recognition service. We do not store recordings.

Sharing

We share data only with our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase) to run the app, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • You can export your records from within the app (CSV/Excel).
  • You can ask us to delete your account and company data at any time by contacting us below. Deletion requests are handled within a reasonable time.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and per-company access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Children

The app is intended for business use and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
